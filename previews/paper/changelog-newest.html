Flux Icons v1.0.0: 1,000 icons in stroke, two-tone, duotone and fill, each rounded and sharp: 8,000 total

Released · 18 Sept 2026

149 drawings added since 0.9.0, bringing the set to 1000, and 59 redrawn.

Four styles

Every name now comes in four styles, stroke, two-tone, duotone and fill, and each style in two corner shapes, rounded and sharp: 8,000 SVGs across 1,000 names. The outlined duotone is called two-tone from this release, so an import of duotone that wants the old look changes to two-tone. Duotone is a new style with no outline, a grey body with the part that matters in black, drawn icon by icon.
